# Feedback form spec — Home Teaching Companion Kit v1.0.0

*Live form: https://tally.so/r/D4QLKp — built by Daniel on Tally from this spec. Follows the cross-kit feedback standard (see the companion-kit-builder skill's `references/feedback-form-spec.md`): 7 questions in the standard order, tenure-neutral summary, hints on every open-text question, standard thank-you. Tone: fun but proper — warm school spirit, professional wording.*

## Form title

"How would you grade your Teaching Assistant?"

## Form description (summary)

"A quick report card for your teaching assistant — 2 minutes, anonymous, and your answers go straight to Daniel, the kit's author, who reads every single one. Thank you!"

## Q1 (required) — Overall rating

Overall, how helpful is your teaching companion so far?

- 1–5 rating scale, required. Labels: "Not helpful" / "Very helpful".

## Q2 — What's most helpful (open text, optional)

What's the most helpful thing the companion has done for your homeschooling?

- Long text, optional.
- Hint: "A lesson plan, a compliance answer, a project idea — specifics help more than you think."

## Q3 — What's not working (open text, optional)

What's not working, confusing, or missing?

- Long text, optional.
- Hint: "Be honest — this is how the next version gets planned."

## Q4 — Which parts have you used? (multiple choice, optional)

Which of the companion's capabilities have you used? (Choose all that apply.)

- A. Intake / family and child profiles
- B. Lesson or unit planning
- C. Activity and project ideas
- D. Progress tracking
- E. State compliance help
- F. Curriculum / resource guidance
- G. Haven't really used it yet

## Q5 — One thing you'd change (open text, optional)

If you could change one thing about the kit, what would it be?

- Short text, optional.
- Hint: "Big or small — it's all useful."

## Q6 — Children's ages (multiple choice, optional)

What ages are the children you're homeschooling? (Choose all that apply.)

- A. Under 5
- B. 5–8
- C. 9–12
- D. 13+

## Q7 — How often do you use it? (single choice, optional)

How often do you turn to the companion in a typical week?

- A. Daily
- B. A few times a week
- C. Weekly
- D. Less than weekly

## Closing screen

(no title)

"Thank you — your feedback helps make this kit better for homeschooling families. Daniel reads every response personally and uses them to plan the next version. Class dismissed — you're doing a great job."

## Build checklist

- [ ] 7 pages in the order above + closing screen (one question block per page)
- [ ] Form summary is tenure-neutral (no time-with-kit assumption)
- [ ] Q1 1–5 rating scale set to **required**, labels "Not helpful" / "Very helpful"
- [ ] Hints in place on Q2, Q3, Q5
- [ ] No name/email fields anywhere
- [ ] Closing screen text in place
- [ ] Cover image uploaded
- [ ] No test submissions without Daniel's approval — and delete any test response afterward

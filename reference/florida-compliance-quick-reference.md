# Florida home-education quick reference

**Version:** 1.0.1
**Last verified:** 2026-09-27

*This appendix holds the verified legal facts the companion consults when the user asks about Florida home-education requirements. It is consulted on request — never volunteered in full unprompted. Facts verified 2026-09-27. This is public legal information, not legal advice.*

## Governing law

- **F.S. 1002.41 — "Home education programs"** (established as a school-choice option in 1985; last substantive amendment HB 732, effective July 1, 2018). Official text at the Online Sunshine legislature site; state authority is FLDOE.
- **This module covers F.S. 1002.41 home education ONLY.** Two other legal paths are different statutes with different rules: umbrella private schools (**F.S. 1002.42**) and the PEP scholarship program (**F.S. 1002.395**). Guides conflate all three constantly — fence them off.

## Key definitions

- Compulsory attendance ages: **6–16**. (A student turning 16 during the school year exits only with a signed declaration of intent to terminate enrollment plus an exit interview with a certified counselor.)

## Required subjects

**None named.** The portfolio and annual evaluation are the academic accountability, not a course list.

## Notification procedure

- **One-time notice of intent — not annual.** Within **30 days of establishing** the program, file a signed, written notice with the **district school superintendent** of the county of residence, including the **full legal names, addresses, and birthdates** of all children enrolled.
- The superintendent **"shall accept the notice and immediately register the home education program upon receipt"** and **may not require any additional information or verification** (unless the student joins a district program), may not assign a grade level, and may not put the student's SSN in any district or state database.
- **Anti-regulation clause (2018):** "A school district may not further regulate, exercise control over, or require documentation from parents of home education program students beyond the requirements of this section" unless needed for a district program (1002.41(13)).
- **Termination:** file written notice with the superintendent, along with the annual evaluation, **within 30 days of termination**.

## Annual educational evaluation

The parent chooses one of five options and files a copy **annually** with the superintendent's office:

1. A certified Florida teacher reviews the portfolio and discusses it with the student.
2. Any nationally normed achievement test administered by a certified teacher.
3. A state student assessment test administered by a certified teacher at a district-approved location.
4. Evaluation by a licensed psychologist.
5. Any other valid measurement tool mutually agreed with the superintendent.

- The evaluation must document progress "at a level commensurate with her or his ability."
- The statute says only "annually" — the widely followed reading is the **anniversary of the notice-of-intent filing**, but that is district/association practice, not statutory text. Confirm with the district.
- **Probation:** if the evaluation doesn't show commensurate progress, the superintendent notifies the parent in writing; the parent has **1 year** of remedial instruction; continuation in the program is contingent on progress at year's end.

## Records (required — portfolio)

Maintain: (1) a **log of educational activities made contemporaneously with instruction**, designating reading materials by title; (2) **samples of writings, worksheets, workbooks, or creative materials** used or developed by the student. The parent determines the content, **preserves it for 2 years**, and makes it available for inspection **on 15 days' written notice**.

## Teacher qualifications

None. "The parent is not required to hold a valid regular Florida teaching certificate."

## Graduation & diplomas; Bright Futures

- No state diploma is issued; the parent determines completion and the family issues its own diploma.
- **Bright Futures: home-ed students are expressly eligible** (F.S. 1002.41(5); 1009.531 exempts them from the standard-diploma requirement). Per the August 2026 handbook (for 2026–27 graduates): registered with the district during the **current AND prior academic year**; **no transcript required**; test-score and service-hour requirements the **same as traditional students** (the old higher home-ed thresholds were eliminated in 2016); **100 hours** of volunteer service or paid work documented to the district home-education office. Thresholds rise by graduation year — never quote a threshold as permanent; check the current handbook.
- College admission: eligible for Florida College System institutions (1007.263) and state universities under Board of Governors policies.

## Re-enrolling in public school

State Board Rule **6A-1.09941**: credits accepted at face value where a transcript exists; for home-education students without an official transcript, credits are validated through **performance during the first grading period** (minimum 2.0 GPA at the end of the first grading period for high school; passing grades for middle grades), with alternative validation procedures available.

## Outdated-guide warnings

- Umbrella-school enrollment = **private-school** enrollment under 1002.42, not home education under 1002.41.
- PEP is a scholarship program, not home education.
- Higher Bright Futures test scores for home-ed students — eliminated 2016; guides still listing them are stale.
- "Re-file the notice of intent every year" — false; the notice is once, only the evaluation is annual.
- District-imposed evaluation deadlines beyond "annually" violate the 2018 anti-regulation clause.

## Honest limits

This appendix states public legal facts with pointers to official sources. It is **not legal advice**. The anniversary-date evaluation convention is practice, not statute; check whether any 2025–2026 legislation amended 1002.41 before acting. For edge cases or disputes, the user should check with their school district or an attorney.

# California home-education quick reference

**Version:** 1.0.0
**Last verified:** 2026-09-27

*This appendix holds the verified legal facts the companion consults when the user asks about California home-education requirements. It is consulted on request — never volunteered in full unprompted. Facts verified 2026-09-27. This is public legal information, not legal advice.*

## Governing law

- California has **no "homeschool statute."** Homeschooling is lawful as private schooling — *In re Rachel L.*, 168 Cal.App.4th 1146 (2008): "California statutes permit home schooling as a species of private school education."
- Dominant route: **home-based private school via the Private School Affidavit (PSA)** under **EC §§ 33190, 33191, 48222**. Other routes: private school satellite program (same sections); credentialed-tutor option (EC 48224). Public charters and public independent study are public-school enrollment, not private homeschooling.
- State authority: California Department of Education (CDE).

## Key definitions

- Compulsory attendance ages: **6–18**.
- EC 48222: private full-time day schools must teach in English and offer instruction in "the several branches of study required to be taught in the public schools."

## Required subjects

CDE points families to **EC 51210 (grades 1–6)** and **EC 51220 (grades 7–12)** as the state courses of study:
- **1–6:** English (incl. cursive/handwriting), mathematics, social sciences, science (incl. climate-change content), visual and performing arts, health, PE (at least 200 min per 10 school days).
- **7–12:** English, social sciences (incl. legal system, genocide/Holocaust), foreign language (from grade 7), PE, science, mathematics, visual and performing arts, applied arts, career technical education, automobile driver education.
- Treat these as CDE's stated interpretation — how binding they are on private schools was not settled by any enforcement mechanism or case law found in the research. Check the official source.

## Notification procedure (the Private School Affidavit)

- File the PSA with the Superintendent of Public Instruction (CDE), **under penalty of perjury**, by the school's owner/head — **every year**.
- Statutory window: **"between the first and 15th day of October of each year."** In practice the CDE online system runs **August 1–June 30**; early filing is valid without October re-filing.
- Contents: business names; addresses; records location and custodian; directors'/officers' names and addresses; enrollment by grade, teacher count, coed/boys/girls, boarding; attestation that attendance records, courses of study, and faculty names/addresses/qualifications are maintained and true; criminal-record-summary compliance.
- Parents are **not** required to report to their school district (districts verify by confirming affidavit filing only when they inquire). Filing is **not** state approval or endorsement. A paper "statement in lieu of the affidavit" is permitted.

## Testing

None. CDE FAQ: "While state testing is not required of private schools, parents can test their students" voluntarily.

## Records (required — three categories)

1. An **attendance register** recording clearly every absence of half a day or more during each day school is maintained (EC 48222).
2. The **courses of study** offered (EC 33190(f)(2)).
3. **Faculty names, addresses, and educational qualifications**, maintained at the stated address and attested true and accurate (EC 33190(f)(3)).
- Also: keep a copy of the affidavit at the school. Individual pupil records are only "highly recommended." Guides citing 5 CCR § 11960 are citing charter-school regulations — wrong.

## Teacher qualifications

- PSA route: **none** — "persons capable of teaching" (undefined; no diploma, degree, or credential required).
- Credentialed-tutor route (EC 48224): valid state credential for the grade taught; instruction at least 3 hours/day, 175 days/year, 8 a.m.–4 p.m., in English.
- Fingerprinting expressly excludes "a parent or legal guardian working exclusively with his or her children."

## Graduation & diplomas

Parent-determined. "There is no law or regulation that establishes graduation or diploma requirements for California private schools… You will need to create your own diploma and transcripts." CDE cautions a home-school diploma "may not be generally recognized"; GED/California Proficiency Program are alternatives. Note: pupils in home-based private schools are **exempt from school immunization requirements** (H&S Code 120335(f)).

## Re-enrolling in public school

Public school districts set their own placement policies and **may refuse to recognize credits** the parent conferred. The former school must transfer the pupil's permanent record on the enrolling school's request (EC 49068).

## Outdated-guide warnings

- Guides citing credential requirements from the **February 2008 *Jonathan L.* opinion** — that opinion was depublished; the **August 2008 published opinion** controls.
- **AB 2756 (2018)** (fire-marshal inspections, address publication, new affidavit data) — **died in committee; not law.**
- "Filing window is October 1–15 only" — outdated in practice; CDE accepts August–June.
- The U.S. Dept. of Ed.'s state-regulation page (~2016) is stale; use CDE/leginfo.

## Honest limits

This appendix states public legal facts with pointers to official sources. It is **not legal advice**. How binding the 51210/51220 subject lists are on private schools, the legal effect of a late PSA filing, and district verification practice were all flagged as unverified/ambiguous in the 2026-09-27 research — check the official source. For edge cases or disputes, the user should check with their school district or an attorney.

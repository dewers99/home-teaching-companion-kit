# 6–8 supplies & materials reference

**Version:** 1.0.1

*This appendix holds the recommended supplies and materials for 6th through 8th grade (roughly ages 11–14), organized so the companion can build a tailored checklist or shopping list for any budget. Consulted when the parent asks what to buy, gather, or set up — never volunteered in full unprompted. Same three-path format: buy it, a budget tip, and a household alternative. Assumes the family owns the 3–5 basics — this list is what gets added or upgraded.*

## How the companion uses this appendix

- Ask about **budget** before recommending: tight, moderate, or "buy the good stuff." Never assume.
- The theme of this band is **systems**: the student is learning to organize their own work, so supplies should support independence (planner, binder, per-subject notebooks).
- Suggest sourcing in this order: things the family already owns → Buy Nothing / thrift / dollar store → library → buy new.

## Notes for the homeschool (not classroom) context

- Skip classroom-communal bulk items — at home those are household supplies.
- The **planner, index cards, and graph paper** are the highest-leverage purchases in this band — they directly build the independence skills the years are about.
- Buy for the term ahead, not the whole three years. Interests and levels shift fast at this age.

## Safety notes

- Compass points and craft knives are sharp — supervise and store with younger siblings in mind.
- Basic chemistry (vinegar, baking soda, food coloring) is kitchen-safe; anything beyond that, check the materials list first.

## Honest limits

This appendix reflects commonly recommended 6–8 materials and widely shared budget practices — not professional safety advice. Age labels on commercial products exist for a reason; when in doubt, follow them.

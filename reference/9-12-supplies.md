# 9–12 supplies & materials reference

**Version:** 1.0.0

*This appendix holds the recommended supplies and materials for 9th through 12th grade (roughly ages 14–18), organized so the companion can build a tailored checklist or shopping list for any budget. Consulted when the parent asks what to buy, gather, or set up — never volunteered in full unprompted. Same three-path format: buy it, a budget tip, and a household alternative. Assumes the family owns the 6–8 basics — this list is what gets added or upgraded.*

## How the companion uses this appendix

- Ask about **budget** before recommending: tight, moderate, or "buy the good stuff." Never assume.
- The theme of this band is **specialization**: supplies follow the student's courses and direction. A chemistry student and a welding apprentice need very different things — never hand over the whole list.
- Suggest sourcing in this order: things the family already owns → Buy Nothing / thrift / dollar store → library → buy new. For big-ticket items (calculator, laptop), **buy once, buy right**.

## Notes for the homeschool (not classroom) context

- Never buy the whole four years upfront. Buy **per course, per term** — directions change.
- The **graphing calculator and a working laptop** are the two big-ticket items; everything else is cheap.
- Co-ops are the budget superpower in this band: shared lab equipment, bulk buys, group classes.

## Safety notes

- Real chemistry means real safety: glasses on, ventilation, adult present, materials stored away from younger siblings.
- Check age and safety labels on all lab materials.

## Honest limits

This appendix reflects commonly recommended 9–12 materials and widely shared budget practices — not professional safety or purchasing advice. Calculator policies vary by test and course; verify before buying. Age labels on commercial products exist for a reason; when in doubt, follow them.

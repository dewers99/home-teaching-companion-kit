# Florida compliance skill

**Version:** 1.0.1
**Last verified:** 2026-09-27

## When to use

Use this module when the parent asks about Florida home-education requirements: what the law requires, what to file, when the deadlines are, whether they're compliant, or how re-enrollment in public school works. If the parent's state isn't settled yet, or they want the cross-state picture, load `skills/compliance-core/` first. This module shares public legal facts and pointers to official sources only. It never gives legal advice, and for anything edge-case or disputed it tells the parent to check with their school district or an attorney.

## Procedure

1. Determine the situation: starting home education for the first time, the annual evaluation, leaving the program, or a re-enrollment/scholarship question. The answer differs by situation, so nail this down first. Also determine **which of Florida's three legal paths** the family is on — this module covers **F.S. 1002.41 home education** only. Umbrella private schools (F.S. 1002.42) and the PEP scholarship program (F.S. 1002.395) are different statutes with different rules; guides conflate them constantly.
2. Consult `reference/florida-compliance-quick-reference.md` for the exact facts before answering — the module states WHEN to consult it and never volunteers the whole appendix unprompted. Give the parent only the piece they need for their situation.
3. Give the specific answer: what to file, when, where, and what to keep. The core facts to convey (facts researched 2026-09-27; do not embellish):
   - Governing law: **F.S. 1002.41 — "Home education programs"** (home education established as a school-choice option in 1985; last substantive amendment HB 732, effective July 1, 2018). Official text at the Online Sunshine legislature site; state authority is FLDOE.
   - Compulsory attendance ages: **6–16**. (A student turning 16 during the school year exits compulsory attendance only with a signed formal declaration of intent to terminate enrollment plus an exit interview with a certified counselor.)
   - **One-time notice of intent — not annual.** Within **30 days of establishing** the program, file a signed, written notice with the **district school superintendent** of the county of residence, including the **full legal names, addresses, and birthdates** of all children enrolled. The superintendent **"shall accept the notice and immediately register the home education program upon receipt"** and **may not require any additional information or verification** (unless the student joins a district program or service), may not assign a grade level, and may not put the student's SSN in any district or state database.
   - **Anti-regulation clause (2018):** "A school district may not further regulate, exercise control over, or require documentation from parents of home education program students beyond the requirements of this section" unless needed for participation in a district program (1002.41(13)). If a district demands extra paperwork, this is the sentence to quote.
   - **The statute names no required subjects.** The portfolio and annual evaluation are the academic accountability, not a course list.
   - **Annual educational evaluation — the parent's choice of five options:** (1) a certified Florida teacher reviews the portfolio and discusses it with the student; (2) any nationally normed achievement test administered by a certified teacher; (3) a state student assessment test administered by a certified teacher at a district-approved location; (4) evaluation by a licensed psychologist; (5) any other valid measurement tool mutually agreed with the superintendent. File a copy **annually** with the superintendent's office. The statute says only "annually" — the widely followed reading is the **anniversary of the notice-of-intent filing**, but that convention is district/association practice, not statutory text, so present it as the usual practice and tell the parent to confirm with their district.
   - **Probation:** if the evaluation doesn't show progress commensurate with ability, the superintendent notifies the parent in writing; the parent has **1 year** of remedial instruction; continuation in the program is contingent on progress at the end of that year.
   - **Records — a portfolio is required.** Maintain: (1) a **log of educational activities made contemporaneously with instruction**, designating reading materials by title; (2) **samples of writings, worksheets, workbooks, or creative materials** used or developed by the student. The parent determines the content, **preserves it for 2 years**, and makes it available for inspection **on 15 days' written notice** — nothing requires the superintendent to actually inspect it.
   - **No parent qualifications.** "The parent is not required to hold a valid regular Florida teaching certificate."
   - **Termination:** file written notice of termination with the superintendent, along with the annual evaluation, **within 30 days of termination**.
   - Graduation and diplomas: no state diploma is issued; the parent determines completion and the family issues its own diploma. **Bright Futures eligibility — home-ed students are expressly eligible** (F.S. 1002.41(5); 1009.531 exempts them from the standard-diploma requirement). Per the August 2026 handbook for 2026–27 graduates: the student must be registered with the district during the **current AND prior academic year**; **no transcript required**; test-score and service-hour requirements are the **same as traditional students** (the old higher home-ed thresholds were eliminated in 2016); **100 hours** of volunteer service or paid work documented to the district home-education office. Test thresholds rise by graduation year — never quote a threshold as permanent; tell the parent to check the current handbook.
   - College admission: home-ed students are eligible for Florida College System institutions (1007.263) and state universities under Board of Governors policies.
   - Re-enrolling in public school: State Board Rule **6A-1.09941** governs — credits are accepted at face value where a transcript exists; for home-education students without an official transcript, credits are validated through **performance during the first grading period** (minimum 2.0 GPA at the end of the first grading period for high school; passing grades for middle grades), with alternative validation procedures available.
4. Close with the honest-limits line (this is public legal information, not legal advice; edge cases go to the district or an attorney) and, where relevant, the myth corrections: umbrella-school enrollment is **private-school** enrollment under 1002.42, not home education under 1002.41; PEP is a scholarship program, not home education; any guide listing higher Bright Futures test scores for home-ed students is stale (fixed in 2016); "re-file the notice of intent every year" is false (the notice is once; only the evaluation is annual); district-imposed evaluation deadlines beyond "annually" violate the 2018 anti-regulation clause.
5. Watch-outs to re-check before the parent acts: the anniversary-date evaluation convention (practice, not statute); whether any 2025–2026 legislation amended 1002.41; and the current Bright Futures handbook thresholds for the child's graduation year.

## Output

Direct answers to the parent's question. If the AI tool cannot save files, say so plainly and provide any checklist as copy-paste text.

## Boundaries

- Never legal advice. If the question goes beyond the verified facts — disputes with a district, special-education questions, truancy proceedings, or anything adversarial — say so honestly and point the parent to their school district or an attorney.
- Never presents the anniversary-date evaluation deadline as statutory — it is district/association practice.
- Never confuses the three Florida paths: 1002.41 home education, 1002.42 private/umbrella school, 1002.395 PEP. If the parent says "umbrella school," stop and clarify which path they're on before answering.
- Defers record-keeping and progress questions to the progress-tracking module; defers curriculum questions to the curriculum-and-resource-guidance module.

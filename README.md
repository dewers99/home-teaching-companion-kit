# Home Teaching Companion Kit v1.0.0

A pure-markdown companion kit that turns your AI tool of choice into a teaching partner for your homeschool — built for the **parent/teacher**, adaptable to **any age or grade**.

## What it does

- **Guided intake** — builds your family profile, one profile per child, your teaching approach, your goals, and whether you want a Christian faith perspective woven in (your choice at setup; neutral by default)
- **Lesson and unit planning** — daily, weekly, and multi-week plans sized to real household life
- **Activities and projects** — hands-on, low-prep, low-cost ideas from ordinary household materials
- **Progress tracking** — simple per-child logs; children are always tracked separately
- **Multi-state compliance support** — a state-neutral compliance core plus one module per state for nine states (Ohio, Michigan, Indiana, Texas, California, Florida, New York, Missouri, Illinois): notification walkthroughs, deadlines, required subjects, testing/evaluation and record-keeping rules under each state's current law, with honest limits: public legal facts plus official sources, never legal advice
- **Curriculum and resource guidance** — curriculum-neutral recommendations matched to your approach and budget; no bundled curriculum
- **Compliance update checks** — your active state module is re-verified against official sources quarterly or on demand (your choice); findings are reported as confirmed / changed / uncertain and applied only with your explicit approval, without needing a kit update

## What's inside

- `AGENT.md` — the companion persona: who it is, how it talks, boundaries, intake, the conversational survey pattern, update checks, feedback
- `skills/` — sixteen capability modules (intake-and-family-profile, lesson-and-unit-planning, activities-and-projects, progress-tracking, curriculum-and-resource-guidance, compliance-core, compliance-update-checks, ohio-compliance, michigan-compliance, indiana-compliance, texas-compliance, california-compliance, florida-compliance, new-york-compliance, missouri-compliance, illinois-compliance)
- `templates/` — seven blank starter docs (family profile, child profile, lesson plan, weekly plan, progress log, notification checklist, compliance check report)
- `reference/` — nineteen appendices, consulted when asked, never volunteered in full: Ohio, Michigan, Indiana, Texas, California, Florida, New York, Missouri, and Illinois home-education quick references, Pre-K developmental-domains reference, Pre-K supplies & materials reference, K–2 subject reference, K–2 supplies & materials reference, 3–5 subject reference, 3–5 supplies & materials reference, 6–8 subject reference, 6–8 supplies & materials reference, 9–12 subject reference, 9–12 supplies & materials reference
- `INSTALL.md` — setup guides for Muse, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, Cursor/coding agents, and a generic fallback, each with honest limits

## Install

See [INSTALL.md](INSTALL.md). Pick your AI tool, follow its section, and tell the companion: "Install this companion kit and start intake with me."

## Updates

The kit never updates itself. About a week after install (then weekly, monthly, or off — your choice), the companion offers to compare your installed version against the repo's `VERSION` file and show what changed. You apply updates by re-running the install message.

Your state's compliance module is checked separately, on its own schedule: quarterly or on demand, your choice, picked during intake or any time after. In quarterly mode the companion offers to re-verify your state's facts against official sources at the turn of each calendar quarter (January, April, July, October) and reports confirmed / changed / uncertain — changes apply only with your explicit approval, and they never require a kit update. See `skills/compliance-update-checks/`.

## Feedback

The kit includes an anonymous feedback form: [Share feedback on the Home Teaching Companion Kit](https://tally.so/r/D4QLKp) (built by the kit's author from `feedback-form-spec.md`). About two weeks after install, the companion offers it once — skippable, never nagging.

## Requesting a state, country, or territory

The kit covers nine states. If your state, country, or territory isn't one of them, the companion will say so honestly — and offer the kit's state-request form: [Request a state, territory, or country](https://tally.so/r/Y5824z) (built by the kit's author from `state-request-form-spec.md`). Requests are counted; the most-requested places get built first.
